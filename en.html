Bibooh Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · GDPR & Google Play Store Compliant

In short: Bibooh is designed with Privacy by Design at its core. It works offline-first: we run no servers and we neither receive nor sell any personal data. The only third-party services are Google's: optional rewarded videos (AdMob, with your consent), purchases (Google Play) and, if you turn it on, saving your game to your Google Play Games account.

1. Publisher and Data Controller

The mobile application Bibooh is published by Alexandre Barakat, under the trade name Créao Studio:

  • Company: CREAO TECH, being formed (registration expected in early 2027). Its legal name and SIRET number will replace these details once registered.
  • Publishing Director: Alexandre Barakat
  • Privacy & DPO Contact: contact@creao-studio.fr

2. Gameplay Data & Local Storage

All data concerning your virtual pet (Bibooh's custom name, growth stage, XP level, inventory, coins, room layout, and local friend roster) is stored exclusively in private local storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences / LocalStorage).

If Google Drive Auto-Backup is enabled on your Android account, your game data is backed up encrypted by Google just like any system app, without Créao Studio having any visibility into your saves. You can also export and import your save file locally at any time as an encrypted JSON backup.

Google Play Games backup (optional): if you sign in to Google Play Games from Settings, a copy of your game is stored in your Google Play Games account ("Saved Games") so you can restore it on another phone. Google stores this copy; Créao Studio has no access to it. You can delete it from your Google Play Games account.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional coin packs and cosmetic items are processed directly through Google Play Billing. Créao Studio never receives or stores your credit card, bank details, or billing address. Our app only receives a cryptographic purchase token verifying the transaction to grant your in-game items instantly.

Statutory Right of Withdrawal: Under European Consumer Rights regulations (Article L.221-28 13° of the French Consumer Code), digital content delivered and executed immediately upon purchase is non-refundable and waives the statutory 14-day withdrawal period upon confirmation of payment.

4. Optional Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob)

No advertisements are ever forced upon you in Bibooh (zero popups, zero interstitial interruptions, zero thumb-zone banners). Only short rewarded videos are available when you voluntarily choose to tap a reward button (up to 6 times per day maximum) to gain in-game treats or coins.

These ads are served via Google AdMob. Google may process device technical data (model, language, coarse IP address, and advertising ID if permitted) for fraud prevention, measurement, and ad delivery. In Europe, your consent is requested upon initial launch via the official Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) CMP, and you can modify your ad preferences anytime inside Settings → Manage Ad Choices.

For more details on Google's advertising practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Local Proximity Multiplayer (Google Nearby Connections)

Duo mini-games and gift exchanges utilize Google Nearby Connections (Wi-Fi Direct / Bluetooth Low Energy). Communication happens directly peer-to-peer between two phones in the same room without going through any server or requiring an active internet connection.

Only essential gameplay inputs (pet name, appearance, ball moves, selected treat) are exchanged locally with your friend's device. No GPS or physical location data is ever read or recorded.

6. Local Notifications

Cozy well-being alerts (e.g. Bibooh waking up from a nap) are scheduled locally on your device's internal clock without any external notification server. You can disable them at any time in Settings or via Android system settings.

7. Data Deletion

Since all data lives locally, uninstalling Bibooh from your smartphone permanently and immediately deletes all game saves and history. You can also reset your progress at any time within the in-game Settings menu.

8. Your Rights & Contact

Under GDPR and applicable international data privacy laws, you retain full rights to access, rectify, and erase any personal data. For privacy inquiries, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

✉️ Email: contact@creao-studio.fr

9. Children's Privacy

Bibooh is rated PEGI 3 / 13+ on app stores and is intended for teens and young adults. The app does not target or collect information from children under 13.
