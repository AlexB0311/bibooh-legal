Bibooh — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Publisher: Alexandre Barakat, trading as Créao Studio (CREAO TECH, company being incorporated)

Créao Studio does not sell any personal data and keeps no file on players. Two Google services process data when you choose to use them: rewarded videos (Google AdMob) and online games with a code (Google Firebase). Details below.
  • Game data: your progress (the name you gave your Bibooh, level, items, friends) is stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, it is included, as for any app. You can also export your game to a file that only you manage.
  • Purchases: coin packs, expansions and skins are sold by Google Play. Créao Studio receives neither your name nor your payment details; the app only checks with Google Play that the purchase exists.
  • Rewarded videos (Google AdMob): no ad is forced on you. A video only starts if you tap a "Watch a video" button to earn a reward. For these videos, Google AdMob may process the phone's advertising ID, technical information (model, language, approximate IP address) and interactions with the ad, to show, measure and secure ads. In Europe, your consent is asked at first launch (Google form) and you can change it at any time in Settings → "Manage my ad choices". Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Play Games backup: if you sign in to Google Play Games, your game (the same content as the game data above) is saved in your Google Play Games space so you can get it back on another phone. Créao Studio has no access to it. You can decline to sign in: the game works the same, offline.
  • Meetings and two-player games nearby: two nearby phones connect directly (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi, Google Play's "Nearby Connections" service). Only your Bibooh's profile card (name, level, look) and game moves are exchanged, only with your friend's phone, without going through the Internet. Up to Android 12, Android requires the "Location" permission to find nearby phones: the app never reads your location.
  • Online games and visits with a code (Google Firebase): if you create or join an online game, the app opens an anonymous connection to Firebase (Google), with no account, no email and no real name. During the game, a temporary "room", identified by a 5-character code, holds your Bibooh's profile card (name, level, look) and the game moves. It is hosted in Europe (Belgium) and deleted when the game ends or when the host loses the connection. Google also processes technical information needed for the service (IP address, anonymous sign-in ID). If you do not play online, nothing is sent. Firebase's policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Notifications: the daily reminder is scheduled locally on your phone, with no server. You can turn it off in the app's or Android's settings.
  • Analytics: none.
  • Age: the app is intended for people aged 13 and over. Rewarded videos are requested without personalization for all players (age is not asked, so the most protective rule applies).

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or ask a question, by writing to contact@creao-tech.com. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone. Contact: contact@creao-tech.com
